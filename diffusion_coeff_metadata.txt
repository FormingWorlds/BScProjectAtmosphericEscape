this dataset contains 'A' and 's' terms for the binary diffusion coefficient, D_12 = AT^s/n, taken from banks&cockarts[1973] and hunten[1973]
the values are such that the units of D_12 are [cm^2 sec^-1], and they are valid for 250K - 2000K
the first colums provides species information. 'X_in_Y' means that X is the minor constituent in primary system of Y
